# Photography

Sourced from Pexels, whose licence allows free commercial use, permits
modification, and does not require attribution. Recorded here anyway, because
being able to show where a photo came from is worth more than the two minutes
it takes to write down.

Deliberately interiors and detail rather than models pretending to clean. A
stock photo of somebody in a kitchen with a spray bottle is the most
recognisable cliché in this industry, it implies the person is our cleaner
when they are not, and customers read it as a business with no photos of its
own work.

Replace these with real work as it accumulates. A phone photograph of an
apartment we actually cleaned beats any of them.
